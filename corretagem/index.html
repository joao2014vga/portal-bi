🫘 Brascof · Brokerage Statement

Contracts cancelled in Digisul that still carry a commission (e.g. washout). They have no due date in Digisul, so they do not enter a month on their own. If the broker charges one in the statement, use “Add to this month”.

  1. Broker and rate come from the Digisul brokerage queries (brascof_pesq_corretagem_compras / _vendas). A contract with two brokers gives one line per broker.
  2. Purchases enter the month by their due date; the base is the contract total.
  3. Sales enter the month by their shipping date (Data Saída); the base is the sale total.
  4. Individual producer (CPF): Digisul stores the price divided by 1.01, and brokers charge on the full price, so the base is multiplied by 1.01.
  5. Split contracts: only the children (A, B…) count, never the original contract. The duplicate line per branch is removed.
  6. Brokerage = base × rate registered in Digisul. Lines with a rate other than 0.5% are flagged.

Difference = statement amount − (calculated brokerage + manual entries).
